# MyPets Digital Library — Cover Art Direction v1

**Goal:** replace generic/duplicated card imagery with a coherent premium collection that immediately communicates each guide topic.

## Collection system

Every cover should share:
- vertical editorial crop;
- strong real-photography focal point;
- enough negative space for title;
- dark-petrol + cream + restrained emerald/gold collection accents;
- official round MyPets medal/seal;
- collection marker: **Coleção MyPets — Cuidar Melhor**;
- no generic stock-ad look;
- no exaggerated sadness;
- no overlaid paragraphs.

The seal is an **editorial signature**, not the hero.

## Cover title system

### 01 — Cuidados Essenciais com o Seu Cão
**Visual story:** relaxed adult dog with tutor at home; calm morning light; water bowl/lead subtly present.
**Emotion:** competence + care.
**Avoid:** veterinary emergency imagery.

### 02 — Os Primeiros 30 Dias com um Filhote
**Visual story:** puppy arriving/settling into a prepared home with bed, toy and adult hands nearby.
**Emotion:** new beginning + safety.
**Avoid:** chaotic puppy pile / studio prop overload.

### 03 — Treino Gentil
**Visual story:** dog making eye contact with tutor during reward-based training outdoors or in a calm home.
**Emotion:** cooperation, not obedience theatre.
**Avoid:** choke collars, pointing fingers, military heel pose.

### 04 — Guia das Raças / Atlas 64
**Visual story:** editorial composition of several visibly different dogs; natural expressions; balanced diversity of size/type.
**Emotion:** discovery + informed choice.
**Avoid:** fake “winner” podium / compatibility score graphics.

### 05 — Alimentação & Bem-Estar
**Visual story:** healthy dog near measured food/water setup; warm kitchen/home context.
**Emotion:** everyday care + clarity.
**Avoid:** ingredient piles implying unsupported nutritional superiority.

### 06 — Linguagem Corporal Canina
**Visual story:** expressive close-up/full-body dog with readable posture and eyes; clean negative space.
**Emotion:** “learn to listen without words”.
**Avoid:** only aggressive/snarl imagery.

### 07 — Passeios sem Stress
**Visual story:** dog and tutor walking on a loose lead in a calm green/urban route.
**Emotion:** freedom + connection.
**Avoid:** perfectly staged heel or taut lead.

### 08 — Ficar Sozinho em Casa
**Visual story:** relaxed dog resting independently near bed/window after owner departure.
**Emotion:** autonomy + calm.
**Avoid:** sad dog staring through a door.

### 09 — Cão em Apartamento
**Visual story:** happy dog in a tasteful compact urban apartment, balcony/window safely managed.
**Emotion:** small space, full life.
**Avoid:** cramped/caged feeling.

### 10 — Higiene, Banho e Saúde Oral
**Visual story:** cooperative grooming/tooth care with dog relaxed and tutor hands visible.
**Emotion:** care + trust.
**Avoid:** forced restraint or overly clinical imagery.

### 11 — Adotei um Cão Adulto: E Agora?
**Visual story:** adult dog walking into/settling in a new home beside adopter.
**Emotion:** second beginning + trust.
**Avoid:** shelter bars / pity-first messaging.

### 12 — Cães e Crianças
**Visual story:** child and dog sharing space with visible adult supervision and respectful distance.
**Emotion:** family + safety.
**Avoid:** face-to-face hugging, child lying on dog, unsupervised contact.

### 13 — 50 Ideias para Enriquecer a Vida do Seu Cão
**Canonical editorial title:** 50 Ideias de Enriquecimento para Cães
**Consumer card title:** 50 Ideias para Enriquecer a Vida do Seu Cão
**Visual story:** engaged dog using nose/problem-solving with simple enrichment objects; dynamic but not chaotic.
**Emotion:** curiosity + play.
**Avoid:** generic running dog that does not communicate mental stimulation.

## Text hierarchy

Top:
**Coleção MyPets — Cuidar Melhor**

Middle:
Guide title, max 3–5 lines.

Bottom:
Short benefit line + round MyPets seal.

Do not put price on the permanent cover asset. Price belongs to the sales card/UI so covers remain reusable.

## Production sizes

Create master:
- 1600 × 2400 px, 2:3 vertical

Derivatives:
- 800 × 1200 cards;
- 1200 × 630 campaign/OG crops;
- square social crop where required.

## Logo

Use the official round MyPets logo already registered as:
`mypets-round-logo`

Never redraw it with generative AI. Composite the official logo after generating/editing the background photography.

## AI-generation rule

AI may generate the editorial background/scene.

The following must be applied afterwards using deterministic design/compositing:
- exact guide title;
- exact MyPets logo;
- collection marker;
- legal/campaign text.

This avoids misspelled text and distorted brand marks.
