# MyPets Content Standard v1

## Editorial objective

Every guide must feel like a useful digital product rather than a thin reward PDF.

Target:
- standard guide: ~5,000–7,000 useful words
- flagship guide: ~7,000–10,000+ useful words where the topic supports it
- short paragraphs and clear headings
- practical actions, examples, checklists and trackers
- no artificial padding

## Research rule

Research widely; publish original MyPets prose.

"Free to read" is not "free to copy". Facts and recommendations can be researched and rewritten. Protected expression, charts, illustrations and long passages must not be reproduced without an appropriate license or permission.

Preferred sources:
- veterinary associations and consensus guidelines
- recognized animal-welfare organizations
- veterinary manuals
- official breed standards
- primary/peer-reviewed literature when a claim requires it

## Medical boundary

MyPets may:
- explain routine care and prevention concepts
- help tutors observe and organize information
- describe general red flags
- encourage professional evaluation

MyPets must not:
- diagnose
- prescribe medication/dose
- prescribe therapeutic diets
- provide an individualized vaccination protocol
- replace emergency veterinary care

## Training standard

Default methodology:
- reward-based
- humane
- gradual progression
- environmental management
- no fear, pain, intimidation, choking or shock as routine training tools

## Required semantic patterns

A chapter should normally include some of:
- Why this matters
- What to observe
- What to do
- Common mistakes
- Practical exercise/checklist
- When to seek professional help
- Sources

Web components to support later:
- KeyPoint
- DoToday
- Checklist
- CommonMistake
- Warning
- VetBoundary
- Example
- ComparisonTable
- ProgressExercise
- VideoEmbed
- MediaCredit
- ImpactCTA

## Tone

Primary locale: pt-BR.

Style:
- clear, warm and practical
- no infantilization
- explain jargon on first use
- avoid absolute promises
- never shame a tutor
- distinguish general education from individualized care

## AI/FacePets tags

Each guide/section may declare:
- ai_use: allowed | restricted | blocked
- medical_risk: none | low | medium | high
- child_safe: true | false
- persona_use: companion | educational | none

Future conversational systems must restrict high-risk health content to navigation/escalation rather than personalized clinical advice.
