# MyPets Content Standard v1

## Editorial objective

A MyPets guide must feel like a useful digital product, not a thin promotional PDF.

The primary release language is Brazilian Portuguese. Prose should be practical, calm, direct and easy to scan on mobile.

## Depth

- standard guide: approximately 5,000–7,000 useful words;
- flagship guide: approximately 7,000–10,000+ useful words where the topic supports it;
- no padding for page count.

Each guide should include practical assets such as checklists, trackers, plans, decision tables or printable worksheets.

## Research and originality

Research widely, publish original MyPets prose.

“Free to read” does not mean “free to copy”. Reuse third-party text, images, charts or video only when license/embedding terms permit it. Otherwise cite/link and rewrite the factual substance.

For health, nutrition, vaccination and behavior prefer recognized veterinary associations, professional guidelines, reputable welfare organizations, veterinary manuals and consensus/primary sources where useful.

## Health boundary

Content may explain routine care, prevention concepts, observations, red flags and questions to take to a professional.

Content must not diagnose, prescribe medication/dose, create an individualized vaccine schedule, prescribe therapeutic diets or replace urgent veterinary evaluation.

## Behavior and training

Default to humane, reward-based training, gradual progression and environmental management. Avoid methods that rely on fear, pain, force or intimidation.

## Web-first components

Suggested semantic blocks:
- KeyPoint
- DoToday
- Checklist
- CommonMistake
- Warning
- VetBoundary
- Example
- ComparisonTable
- Tracker
- SourceNote
- ImpactCTA

The same content should render well both on the website and in PDF.

## Source ledger

Each guide maintains a source ledger containing publisher, title, URL, access date, source type, topic and reuse note.

## Media

Prefer:
1. original MyPets photography/illustration;
2. public-domain or clearly licensed media;
3. official third-party video embeds/links where embedding is permitted;
4. external links when reuse rights are unclear.

Do not download and republish third-party photos or videos merely because they are publicly viewable.

## AI metadata

Each release can declare:
- ai_use: allowed | restricted | blocked
- medical_risk: none | low | medium | high
- child_safe: true | false
- persona_use: companion | educational | none
