# MyPets Digital Library — Premium Pre-Launch Editorial Audit

**Scope:** 13 guides · launch candidate  
**Purpose:** decide what is ready to sell now, what must be improved before paid traffic, and what can move to v1.1 without delaying launch.

## Editorial conclusion

The corpus is already strong enough to function as a paid digital library. The main launch risk is **not lack of text**. The weak point is **perceived product value at first glance**: covers, inline media, visual rhythm and premium presentation.

The launch strategy should therefore be:

1. freeze the copy except for high-value editorial fixes;
2. perform targeted veterinary/factual review on sensitive sections;
3. upgrade covers/cards;
4. guarantee a minimum rich-media floor in every guide;
5. launch;
6. render the full 103-placement visual system in v1.1.

## Launch quality bar

A guide is launch-ready when it has:
- a clear promise in the first screen;
- practical actions/checklists;
- safety boundaries where relevant;
- at least one strong cover image;
- at least 2 useful inline visual/media moments;
- a readable print/PDF fallback;
- no internal production directives visible;
- no unsupported medical claim.

## Highest-value fixes before paid traffic

### 01 — Cuidados Essenciais
Keep content stable. Add:
- daily rhythm visual;
- home-safety map;
- red-flag card;
- stronger topic-specific cover.

### 02 — Primeiros 30 Dias
Content is strong, but health/socialisation boundaries deserve final professional review. Add:
- first-72-hours timeline;
- 30-day roadmap;
- puppy-specific cover.

### 03 — Treino Gentil
One of the strongest launch guides. Add:
- 2–3 official training video embeds;
- 3D difficulty visual;
- command-progress printable.

### 04 — Atlas 64
Do not add more prose before launch. At ~9k words, density is already high.
UX improvement:
- long-form intro + separate searchable/filterable atlas;
- breed photography grid;
- filters by size/energy/grooming/trainability;
- no deterministic compatibility score.

### 05 — Alimentação & Bem-Estar
Prioritise factual review over expansion.
Add:
- label-reading visual;
- food-transition visual;
- body-condition media;
- tracker.

### 06 — Linguagem Corporal
Strong practical guide. Visual content will dramatically raise perceived value.
Add:
- annotated whole-dog figure;
- traffic-light;
- real body-language examples.

### 07 — Passeios sem Stress
Launch-ready. Use video and route visuals to avoid text-only feel.

### 08 — Ficar Sozinho
Strong and responsible, but visually abstract.
Add:
- seconds-to-minutes staircase;
- camera observation board;
- 14-day progress calendar.

### 09 — Cão em Apartamento
Strong urban-life utility. Add:
- functional-zone floor plan;
- noise map;
- weekly routine planner.

### 10 — Higiene & Saúde Oral
Strong practical content. Final dental claims should be professionally spot-checked.
Add:
- toothbrushing steps;
- body tolerance map;
- grooming tools/real photo.

### 11 — Cão Adulto
Emotionally useful and practical. Cover should communicate “new beginning”, not generic rescue sadness.

### 12 — Cães e Crianças
Good safety framing. Add:
- five-rule poster;
- dog traffic light;
- safe-space diagram.
Avoid marketing imagery with unsupervised child/dog hugging.

### 13 — Enriquecimento
The canonical title **is correct and explicitly canine**:
**“50 Ideias de Enriquecimento para Cães”**.

The problem was the abbreviated commerce/card label **“50 Ideias de Enriquecimento”**, which is too abstract for a cold audience.

Preferred consumer-facing short title:
**“50 Ideias para Enriquecer a Vida do Seu Cão”**

Preferred sales subtitle:
**“Brincadeiras, farejo, desafios mentais e atividades simples para gastar energia com qualidade.”**

## Minimum visual floor for launch

Before buying paid traffic, every guide should expose at least:
1. topic-specific cover/card;
2. one real photograph or official video;
3. one MyPets original visual/checklist;
4. one strong practical takeaway card.

That is enough for launch.

The full 103-placement map remains the v1.1 target.

## Professional review P0

Prioritise:
- Guide 02: vaccination/socialisation wording;
- Guide 05: toxic foods, diets, supplements, body condition;
- Guide 10: dental/oral-health claims;
- Guide 12: child/dog safety;
- emergency/red-flag statements across Guide 01.

## Do not add before launch

Do not create more guides, more breed profiles or substantially more word count before the first paid-traffic cohort.

The current constraint is **presentation and validation**, not content volume.
